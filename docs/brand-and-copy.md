# Brand and copy

This guide keeps the public site, Prumo Web, Prumo Local, documentation, screenshots, and social assets aligned around one product idea.

## Positioning

### Primary line

> **Know where you stand. Keep it yours.**

This line connects the product job to the meaning of the name. It works for both browser-local files and the SQLite edition without making an implementation claim by itself.

### Literal description

> Prumo is a private, local-first net worth tracker. Open your spreadsheet in the browser with no account, bank connection, or financial-data upload — or run Prumo locally with SQLite and verified backups.

Use the primary line and literal description together. The first creates a point of view; the second makes the product and its two modes immediately understandable.

### Supporting lines

- **Your file. Your browser. Your data.** — use as the Prumo Web privacy proof line.
- **Bring your spreadsheet. Leave with clarity.** — use around import and the template.
- **Record snapshots, not every coffee.** — use to explain the model, not as the main privacy claim.
- **Net worth tracking without the bank connection.** — use when literal search or comparison context is more important than distinctiveness.

Do not use **“A finance app that never sees your finances”** as an unqualified technical claim. The software necessarily processes financial data locally. Prefer **“We do not receive your financial data”**, **“No financial data is uploaded”**, or **“Processed in this browser”** where the implementation supports the statement.

## Core narrative

Most finance products ask people to create an account, connect a bank, and continuously provide transaction data. Prumo asks for less because its job is smaller: record what someone has at useful moments and show how that position changes.

The progression is:

```text
Try a fictional demo
        ↓
Bring a spreadsheet or start with the template
        ↓
Keep working in the browser and export at any time
        ↓
Run Prumo Local for SQLite and verified backups
```

Prumo Web is not a trial that leads to an account. Prumo Local is not a cloud upgrade. Both are complete local-first choices at different persistence levels.

## Name

*Prumo* is Portuguese for a plumb line — a simple reference that shows whether something is truly aligned.

The preferred short explanation is:

> Prumo is Portuguese for a plumb line — a simple reference that shows whether something is truly aligned. It helps you know where you stand.

## Visual direction

The mark should be a reduced plumb-line symbol: a vertical reference stroke with a clear anchor and a pointed weight. It must remain identifiable in one colour at favicon size.

The existing warm off-white, near-black, and evergreen palette fits the product: calm, legible, and distinct from conventional banking blue. Keep the restrained borders, generous space, large tabular numbers, and equivalent light and dark themes.

A fine vertical rule can become a quiet layout motif in the landing page and social assets. Motion, if used, should be limited to a subtle settling or alignment gesture and must respect reduced-motion preferences.

Do not use coins, currency symbols, bank buildings, wallets, credit cards, upward-growth arrows, or stock-chart silhouettes as brand marks. Product screenshots may naturally contain formatted currency and charts; the logo should not.

## Public image system

- show the real interface with the repository's fictional demo data;
- label demo data visibly;
- make the headline total and chart selection reconcile in the first hero capture;
- favour direct product crops over device mockups;
- keep light and dark captures equally considered;
- never use personal data, paths, browser chrome, notifications, or fake activity metrics.

The social preview should combine the plumb-line mark, the primary line, and a real demo dashboard crop. It should not display fake GitHub stars, user counts, savings claims, or performance figures.

## Voice

Prumo speaks plainly, calmly, and precisely.

- lead with the user outcome, then show the technical proof;
- prefer short sentences and familiar financial language;
- explain “client-side,” “IndexedDB,” and “SQLite” only where they establish a useful boundary;
- say what Prumo does not collect or upload, not that it is magically private;
- distinguish Prumo Web from Prumo Local whenever storage or networking is discussed;
- describe Privacy Mode as visual concealment, never encryption;
- avoid urgency, fear, lifestyle promises, and institutional finance language.

## Calls to action

The public landing page uses:

- **Open Prumo** — primary;
- **View on GitHub** — secondary;
- **Run locally** — in the Prumo Local section.

Inside Prumo Web, the first-run choices are:

- **Open your file** — “Choose an XLSX or CSV. It is processed on this device.”
- **Start with a template** — “Begin with a simple spreadsheet you own.”
- **Explore demo** — “See Prumo with clearly fictional data.”

The privacy reassurance should sit beside the action it qualifies, not only on a separate policy page.
